ATC Transcription Challenge

Test your listening skills on 5 real air traffic control audio recordings. Your transcriptions will be automatically graded and compared against the ground truth.

Quiz Rules & Guidelines

  • Listen to each recording (5 total). You can listen as many times as you like.
  • Type the exact words you hear. Transcribe numbers as words (e.g., "two one left" instead of "21L" or "21 left") and format callsigns clearly.
  • Speaker Segmentation: You must start a new segment every time the speaker changes. Press Enter or click + Add Segment to split.
  • There is no speaker role assignment (e.g. Pilot or ATC) or timestamping required.
  • Adjust playback speed at any time using the range slider or hotkeys.
  • Your score is based on the word-level accuracy of your segments compared side-by-side with the ground truth.
